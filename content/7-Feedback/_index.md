During my internship at AWS/First Cloud Journey from April 2026 to July 2026, I had a lot of learning experiences, training, and applying knowledge in practice. This program was very helpful in concentrating on internship tasks, and I was able to apply the skills on many different topics. Below are the things I learned and gained from the internship program:

### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**  
The working environment is open and highly suitable for beginners learning basic AWS services. The seniors at FCAJ are always ready to support us when we face difficulties, even outside of working hours. The workspace is well-organized and comfortable, which greatly enhances focus. The program is primarily driven by self-directed learning and independent research, supported by a learning community across multiple social networks, which helped me develop strong independent working skills.

**2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin**  
The admin team actively assists with administrative procedures and addresses our questions and challenges, empowering us to handle and resolve issues on our own. Instead of simply handing out the answers, the FCAJ admins consistently guide us toward the root cause of the problems so we can figure out the solutions independently.

**3. Relevance of Work to Academic Major**  
As a Software Engineering major, my previous knowledge was mostly limited to basic coding and deployment. Thanks to the FCAJ program and learning about AWS, I have expanded my expertise in AWS cloud services, application deployment, and designing robust system architectures.

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**  
Beyond AWS knowledge, I significantly improved my soft skills, including teamwork, writing worklogs, presenting progress, and delegating tasks. Working on the *NatuEra - Green Banking* project provided me with substantial practical experience that will help guide my future career path.

**5. Company Culture & Team Spirit**  
The company culture fosters a strong team spirit, mutual respect, creativity, self-directed learning, and a continuous drive for improvement.

### What I Am Most Satisfied With During the Internship

What I appreciate the most is that FCAJ provided me with an exceptional working environment and the opportunity to learn and connect with peers from different universities. It broadened my knowledge, practical experience, and real-world working skills that simply cannot be acquired solely within a traditional university setting.

### Conclusion
I believe that FCAJ is a highly recommended program for anyone looking to learn about AWS in particular, and cloud services in general. The practical working environment at FCAJ helped me stay highly focused throughout my internship, and the knowledge I gained is applicable across multiple platforms.
